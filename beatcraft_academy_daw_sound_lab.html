SOUND DESIGN & SYNTHESIS

The "Wub Wub" LFO Bass Masterclass

The iconic "Wub Wub" wobble bass is created by a Low-Frequency Oscillator (LFO) cycling a resonant low-pass filter on a harmonically rich sawtooth or square waveform.

🔊 Interactive Wobble Rig

Audition the live filter sweep and adjust LFO rate and resonance.

1

The Raw Waveform

Sine waves have no overtones. You must start with detuned Sawtooth or Square waves rich in harmonics.

2

Resonant Filter (Q)

A 24dB Low-Pass filter with resonance boosted creates that talking squelch at the cutoff point.

3

Tempo-Synced LFO

The LFO modulates the cutoff in rhythm: 1/8 bounce for walking grooving, 1/8 triplets for dub tension.

AFRICAN ELECTRONIC PRODUCTION

The Durban Gqom Masterclass

Originating in Durban, South Africa, Gqom replaced standard 4-on-the-floor house with raw, syncopated broken rhythms, pitch-bent low toms, and modal drones.

1

The Broken 3-Kick / Tresillo Pulse

Kicks hit on Step 1, Step 7, and Step 11 (3+3+2 syncopation) creating forward lean without rushing.

2

Pitch-Bent Low Thud Tom

A low-tuned tom dropping from 110 Hz to 46 Hz hits on off-beat steps 4 and 13 as a dark counter-dialogue to the kick.

3

Metallic Clacks & Rims

Sharp metallic woodblocks and FM rimshots replacing standard claps, accenting step 7, 23, and rapid turnaround rolls.
